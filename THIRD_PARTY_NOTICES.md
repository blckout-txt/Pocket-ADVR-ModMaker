## ADVR Modding Tools — Lua definitions

`app/src/main/assets/advr_api.txt` is generated by `tools/gen_api_index.py` from the LuaLS
definition stubs that ship with the **ADVR Modding Tools** Visual Studio Code extension. It is a
re-encoding of those stubs — the same class, field and method declarations in a compact form the
Android app can parse quickly — and is redistributed here under the extension's MIT license.
